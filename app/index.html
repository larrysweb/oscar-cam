About

Oscar Cam 0.1.0 — a free, LAN-only camera grid for webOS TV. No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Camera credentials stay on the TV.

Playback credits: VideoRTC by AlexxIT (go2rtc project, MIT) for go2rtc and WebRTC tiles; hls.js for HLS tiles. Proven on the C3: 9 simultaneous go2rtc substreams, smooth. Raw RTSP is not playable in the TV browser — use go2rtc or a camera-native browser-playable stream.
